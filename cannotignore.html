Cannot Ignore

Critical alarms you can't miss

Never miss another important moment. Cannot Ignore delivers full-screen alarms designed specifically for those critical, one-time events that demand your full attention.

When your alarm fires, Cannot Ignore takes over your entire screen with unmissable alerts. Import events from Calendar or Reminders with automatic link detection for video conferences, set custom alarm sounds, and access meeting links instantly when alarms fire.

Perfect for crucial meetings, appointments, flight departures, and deadlines you absolutely cannot afford to miss.

Key Features

Unmissable Alerts

  • Full-screen alarms that take over your device
  • Custom audio file support for unique alarm sounds
  • Real-time alarm status updates with color-coded icons
  • In-app alarm control and monitoring
  • Edit existing alarms anytime

Smart Integration

  • Import from Calendar events with automatic link detection
  • Import from Reminders with automatic link detection
  • Detects video conference links (Zoom, Meet, Teams)
  • One-tap access to links when alarms fire
  • Automatically includes event notes and URLs

Perfect For

  • Job interviews and important meetings
  • Medical appointments
  • Flight departures and travel
  • Deadline submissions
  • Video conference calls
  • Time-sensitive commitments

Privacy Focused

At Cephalopod Studio, your privacy comes first. Cannot Ignore is designed with privacy at its core.

  • No Data Collected: The developer does not collect any data from this app.
  • No Third-Party Tracking: We do not use any third-party trackers or analytics.
  • Your Data Stays Yours: Your alarms and settings remain private on your devices.
  • Local Storage Only: All alarm data is stored locally using iOS AlarmKit.

Calendar and Reminders access is used only for the import feature and no data leaves your device.
