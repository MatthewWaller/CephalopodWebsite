Passage

Fully immersive worlds for work and play on Apple Vision Pro

Get focused work done in wondrous places. Visit fully 3D worlds or generate any place with AI. Feel productive; feel at ease; feel awed.

Download on the App Store

App Store Details

Features

  • AI-generated or artist-made immersive 3D worlds
  • Upload your own places or choose from curated options
  • Spatial computing cutout to see your desk, keyboard, coffee
  • In-app browser for web browsing, streaming video and music
  • Rich text editor with export capabilities (.rtf)
  • Focus mode for distraction-free writing
  • Rotate worlds to choose your perfect vista
  • Support for multiple windows

Compatibility

  • Apple Vision Pro: Requires visionOS 2.0 or later
