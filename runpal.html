RunPal

Your Crew. Every Mile.

Run together on a shared live map with real voice cheers and haptic buzzes. Connect with friends and community members in real time, no matter where they are, for a connected running experience.

Initiate group runs in one tap, host community events, send mid-run voice messages that auto-duck your music, and stay accountable with friend nudges and an AI coach that learns your schedule.

Download on the App Store

App Store Details

Features

  • Live shared run map with real-time crew visibility
  • Voice message cheers up to 15 seconds, with music auto-ducking
  • Haptic buzz notifications from friends
  • One-tap group run creation
  • Community event hosting and participation
  • Friend nudge system for accountability
  • AI fitness coach that learns your schedule
  • GPS tracking for distance, pace, and time

Compatibility

  • iPhone: Requires iOS 26.0 or later
  • iPad: Requires iPadOS 26.0 or later
  • Mac: Requires macOS 26.0 or later (M1 or later)
  • Apple Vision: Requires visionOS 26.0 or later
