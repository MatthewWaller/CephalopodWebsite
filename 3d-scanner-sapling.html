3D Scanner: Sapling

Photogrammetry & LiDAR scanning for iOS, macOS, and Apple Vision Pro

Make 3D models for augmented reality, 3D printing, game development, or simply sharing with friends.

It performs photogrammetry on images with LIDAR enabled scanning, or you can use AI generate objects from text. Export in a variety of 3D quality, including high-end textures. All photogrammetry processing happens on your device!

Download on the App Store

App Store Details

Features

  • Photogrammetry from camera images
  • LiDAR scanning (on supported devices)
  • Export in USDZ, OBJ, STL, and PLY formats
  • High-quality textures
  • Local processing (no cloud required)
  • No subscriptions needed

Compatibility

  • iPhone: Requires iOS 17.0 or later
  • iPad: Requires iPadOS 17.0 or later
  • Mac: Requires macOS 15.0 or later
  • Apple Vision Pro: Requires visionOS 2.0 or later

Privacy Policy

At Cephalopod Studio, we take your privacy seriously. 3D Scanner: Sapling does not collect any user data. All processing happens locally on your device.

The app does not require an account, does not track your usage patterns, and does not send any information to our servers or third parties. Your 3D models and scans remain completely private and under your control at all times.

This app complies with Apple's App Privacy standards and has disclosed to Apple that it does not collect any user data.
