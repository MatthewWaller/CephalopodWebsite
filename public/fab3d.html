Fab3D

Your 3D Model Library

Fab3D is the ultimate space for 3D enthusiasts, designers, and creators. Browse, collect, and share stunning 3D models from the Sketchfab community, all in one beautifully designed app.

Whether you're a professional 3D artist, hobbyist, or simply appreciate beautiful 3D creations, Fab3D transforms how you discover and interact with 3D content. Your entire 3D model collection, always at your fingertips.

Download on the App Store

App Store Details

Features

  • Discover thousands of high-quality 3D models from talented creators worldwide
  • Save favorites to your personal Likes collection
  • Download models for offline viewing
  • Upload USDZ, OBJ, STL, and other 3D creations to Sketchfab
  • Built-in 3D viewer — rotate, zoom, and explore every angle
  • AR-ready: view models in your real-world environment
  • Organize and share with friends, colleagues, or clients

Compatibility

  • iPhone: Requires iOS 18.6 or later
  • Mac: Requires macOS 15.6 or later (M1 or later)
  • Apple Vision: Requires visionOS 2.6 or later
