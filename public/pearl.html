Pearl: Meaningful Reminders

Motivation, Wellness, Affirm

Make meaningful reminders that appear at random during time periods of your choosing. Reminders can sync across devices & display images. Add widgets!

Create reminders that mean a lot to you, that bring you joy comfort and rest. You choose the time frame, and a number of reminders that you select will appear randomly during that period. You can even choose an image to go with your notifications, which can sync across devices.

Enjoy a beautiful tong notification sound and your hand-crafted reminder. The background animates subtly, respecting accessibility choices.

App Store Details

Features

  • Create custom meaningful reminders
  • Reminders appear randomly within chosen timeframes
  • Sync reminders & images across devices (iPhone, iPad, Mac, Watch, Vision)
  • Attach images to notifications
  • Home Screen & Lock Screen Widgets
  • Apple Watch app with complication
  • Subtle animated background
  • Light and Dark Mode support
  • Customizable notification titles & multiple app icons
  • Option to disable suggestions & word count
  • Respects Reduce Motion & Transparency settings

Compatibility

  • iPhone: Requires iOS 17.0 or later
  • iPad: Requires iPadOS 17.0 or later
  • Mac: Requires macOS 14.0 or later
  • Apple Watch: Requires watchOS 10.0 or later
  • Apple Vision: Requires visionOS 1.0 or later

Privacy Focused

At Cephalopod Studio, your privacy comes first. Pearl: Meaningful Reminders is designed with privacy at its core.

  • No Data Collected: The developer does not collect any data from this app.
  • No Third-Party Tracking: We do not use any third-party trackers or analytics.
  • Your Data Stays Yours: Your reminders and settings remain private on your devices or synced via your private iCloud account.

You can review the full privacy policy on the App Store or the developer's website.
