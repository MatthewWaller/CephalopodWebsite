Novel Writing: Candlelight

Write amazing stories with a Story Bible and smart assistance

Write works of fiction with the help of Candlelight. This app aims to respect both writers who just want to write, with no bells and whistles, but also creatives who want to explore and tinker with what AI can do.

Download on the App Store

App Features

Level 1: No AI

  • Story Bible with sections for characters, themes, world building, and plot outlines
  • Fully-featured rich text editor
  • Convert your book into a PDF
  • Organize your work into chapters or sections
  • Full-screen writing experience with removable sidebars

Level 2: Some AI

  • Generate content for each section of the Story Bible
  • Narrative autocomplete that understands your entire story context
  • Get unblocked with AI-powered writing suggestions

Level 3: Full AI

  • Create an entire novelette automatically
  • Generate content based on your Story Bible
  • Use as a starting point for further development

Compatibility

  • iPhone: Requires iOS 18.0 or later
  • iPad: Requires iPadOS 18.0 or later

Privacy Policy

We use third party analytics to help us improve the app.

We do not sell any of your data.

Your writing is sent to third-party LLM vendors for the generative components for context, though we've ensured that permission to train on your data is denied.
