Journal Diary Rambler

Hands-free journaling for iOS 26

Rambler is a hands-free journaling app that lets you capture your thoughts out loud during walks, commutes, or chores. All transcription happens on-device using iOS 26 APIs, keeping your private reflections truly private.

Download on the App Store

Key Features

Hands-Free Voice Journaling

  • Speak your thoughts naturally during walks or chores
  • On-device transcription using iOS 26 APIs
  • No Apple Intelligence required
  • Supports 10+ languages and 35+ regional variants

Intelligent Prompts

  • AI-generated prompts based on your current entry
  • 150+ curated prompts for daily logging, emotions, and creative writing
  • Spoken aloud using on-device text-to-speech
  • Voice commands: "Next prompt" or "Stop session"

Rich Attachments

  • Add photos, videos, and audio clips
  • Tag locations and moods
  • iOS Journal API integration for memories and activities
  • Rich text formatting with iOS 26

Privacy-First AI Features

  • Auto-generate bullet point summaries (optional)
  • Automatic title generation (optional)
  • Mood detection (optional)
  • All AI features can be disabled for 100% privacy

Data Portability

  • Export entries individually or in bulk
  • All attachments included in exports
  • Own your data completely

Platform Support

  • Requires iOS 26 or later
  • Designed for both iPhone and iPad

Privacy & Transparency

At Cephalopod Studio, we take your privacy seriously. All transcription in Rambler happens entirely on-device using iOS 26 APIs. Your private reflections stay on your device.


Optional AI features (summaries, titles, mood detection) can be completely disabled for a 100% private experience. When enabled, these features also run on-device whenever possible.


The iOS Journal API integration preserves privacy by design—you must manually select memories and activities through Apple's journaling suggestions picker. We cannot access this data without your explicit action.


Your data is yours. Export your entries and attachments anytime, in full.
