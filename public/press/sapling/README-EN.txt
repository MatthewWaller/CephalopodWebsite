SAPLING: PRESS KIT
3D Scanner: Sapling, by Cephalopod Studio
App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/3d-scanner-sapling/id6450019198
Contact: Matthew Waller, hello@cephalopod.studio
Deutsche Version: https://www.cephalopod.studio/press/sapling/

Sapling turns real-world objects into detailed 3D models, right on iPhone, iPad and Mac. With LiDAR you walk once around an object and Apple's Object Capture builds the model on the device, with no servers. Without LiDAR, Sapling takes photos automatically on a turntable; process them on the iPhone or in the free Mac app. Place models in AR, set their real-world height, and export STL, OBJ, PLY or USDZ. iCloud sync, including to Apple Vision Pro. Version 2.2 arrives on November 3, 2026, in German, French, Spanish, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Korean. A pre-release TestFlight build (iPhone, iPad, Mac) is available on request. Free to download, and the first scan is free; the full version is a one-time $19.99.

All images are free to use in coverage of Sapling.
