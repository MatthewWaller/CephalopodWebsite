PEARL: PRESS KIT
Daily Affirmations: Pearl, by Cephalopod Studio
App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/daily-affirmations-pearl/id1567837432
Contact: Matthew Waller, hello@cephalopod.studio

Pearl sends you a few words of encouragement at random moments in your day. Version 3.0 (Oct 6, 2026) adds Strands: groups of Pearls, each with its own schedule. An automatic Strand writes new Pearls on the device with Apple's Foundation Models. iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, with iCloud sync. Optimized for iPhone Duo from Oct 23, 2026. Free to download; $2.99/month, $14.99/year or $19.99 lifetime.

All images are plain captures of the app with no marketing text or device frames, free to use in coverage of Pearl. Every quote and photo shown is sample content.
