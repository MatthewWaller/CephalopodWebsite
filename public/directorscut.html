$ brew tap MatthewWaller/directorscut
$ brew install --cask directorscut

# Add your Gemini API key
$ directorscut setup

# Make your first video
$ directorscut edit -p "30s product demo" -f ./clips/

How it works

1

Point it at your footage

Pass a folder of raw clips. Director's Cut detects scenes, transcribes speech, and builds a visual understanding of every shot.

2

Describe what you want

Write a prompt: "30-second product demo with narration" or "highlight reel of the best action shots." The AI makes the editorial decisions.

3

Get a finished video

Out comes a rendered MP4 with transitions, narration, and subtitles. Publish directly to YouTube or TikTok, or export to your NLE for final polish.
